Luvvy Last Hit

Premium last hit and deny assistant with animation cancelling built for manual farming.

Features

  • ⌁Automatically holds attacks until the predicted last hit or deny window
  • ◆Supports lane creeps, allied denies, neutrals, and under tower farming
  • ↗Learns observed creep and tower damage to improve prediction
  • ◌Fake attack animations that can convert into a last hit
  • ◎Right click targeting or closest to mouse hotkey
  • ▰Subtle health bar state indicator

Guide

  1. 1Enable the script.
  2. 2Choose a creep and issue an attack command with right click. You can also use the target hotkey to grab the creep closest to your mouse.
  3. 3Once the creep reaches a low enough health range, it will secure the last hit automatically.
  4. 4Under tower, it may release attacks early to prepare the final hit. This is not guaranteed for every creep.
