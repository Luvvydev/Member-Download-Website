Luvvy Last Hit

Premium last hit and deny assistant with animation cancelling built for manual farming.

Features

  • ⌁Automatically holds attacks until the predicted last hit or deny window
  • ◆Supports lane creeps, allied denies, neutrals, and under tower farming
  • ↗Predicts incoming creep, tower, and hero attacks while learning observed damage to improve timing
  • ◌Fake attack animations that can convert into a real last hit when the timing becomes ready
  • ◎Right click targeting, closest-to-mouse selection, or a lowest-HP creep selector while held
  • ➜Holding the selector hotkey follows your mouse and automatically takes over movement when a last hit or deny needs range
  • ▰Health bar indicator shows the predicted HP threshold where the script plans to attack
  • ⚔Harasses nearby enemy heroes during safe gaps between last hit windows
  • ×Settable clear-actions hotkey instantly drops the current creep and script-controlled actions
  • ⏱Accounts for attack speed, attack animation, turn time, attack cooldown, and projectile travel

Guide

  1. 1Enable the script and set your creep selector and clear-actions hotkeys.
  2. 2Select a creep with right click, use Closest to mouse mode, or hold the selector hotkey in Lowest HP mode to automatically prefer the lowest-health valid last hit or deny target.
  3. 3While the selector hotkey is held, your hero follows your mouse. If the selected creep is approaching a real farm window and you need to get into attack range, the script temporarily takes over movement toward the creep.
  4. 4When the predicted last hit or deny window is ready, the script commits the attack. Under tower it also accounts for incoming tower and creep damage when deciding when to start the hit.
  5. 5Press the clear-actions hotkey whenever you want to drop the current target and return full control until you select another creep.

Quick tuning

Last hit timing

If hits are landing a little early or late, these two settings are the best place to start.

Too early: move it left. If the creep survives with a small amount of HP, try 98 and adjust by 1 or 2 points at a time.

Too late: move it right. 100 means the script uses 100% of its estimated attack damage.

Recommended: Minimum. It uses the low end of your hero's damage range, which makes the last hit timing more conservative.
